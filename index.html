Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?

Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone at 072 281 6456. We create the account and send you the username and password.

Which grades is it for?

Grade 6, 7, 8, 9 and O/L (Grades 10 and 11). Each grade has its own lessons and a mock exam.

Do we need to install anything?

No. It works in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. You can also add it to your home screen like an app.

Does it follow the O/L English paper?

Yes. The practice papers follow the 16-test format of the O/L English paper, with original questions and a marking guide.

Is it safe for children?

Yes. There is no public sign-up, no ads and no chat with strangers. Teachers control what each student can open.

Can students practise speaking?

Yes. Talk Town lessons and voice games use the microphone, so students can practise speaking and listening.
